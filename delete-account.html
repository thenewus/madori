Delete your Madori account

Madori: Writing & Notes · by thenewus

You can delete your Madori account and its data at any time. Deleting can't be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Madori and make sure you're online.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account…, then Delete Account to confirm.

The account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email madori.app88@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Madori account". We'll reply to confirm it's you, delete the account within 30 days, and let you know when it's done. The result is the same as deleting in the app.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and password hash.
  • Your encryption keys.
  • All your synced pages and folders on the server, including the trash and sync conflict copies.
  • The pages on your devices: on the device you delete from right away, and on your other devices the next time they connect.

What is kept

Only the random id of the deleted account and the date it was deleted, with no email address or content. It's kept so that your devices can find out the account was deleted, even if they were offline at the time. Our service provider's backups may keep deleted data for a limited period before it's removed from them too.
